How to Choose the Right Mobile Hairdresser in Aylesbury

Home›Blog›How to Choose the Right Mobile Hairdresser in Aylesbury

14 March 2026

If you've never used a mobile hairdresser before, choosing the right one can feel like a bit of a leap of faith. You're inviting someone into your home and trusting them with your hair – so it's important to get it right. As a mobile hairdresser serving Aylesbury and Buckinghamshire for over 21 years, I've put together this guide to help you make an informed choice.

Check Their Qualifications and Training

A professional mobile hairdresser should be fully qualified to at least NVQ Level 2 or Level 3 standard in Hairdressing. Don't be afraid to ask about their training background. Many hairdressers also undertake ongoing professional development, learning new techniques in areas like grey blending, balayage, or keratin treatments.

I trained to NVQ Level 3 and have continued developing my skills throughout my career, keeping up with modern techniques and product knowledge to deliver the best possible results for my clients.

Look for Experience Relevant to Your Needs

Different clients need different things. If you're looking for wedding or bridal hair, you need a hairdresser with specific experience in bridal styling and the ability to create styles that last all day. If you need care home or elderly mobile hairdressing, you need someone patient, gentle and experienced in working with clients who have specific accessibility needs.

Ask potential hairdressers about their experience in the area you need. General experience is good, but specialist experience in your specific requirements is even better.

Ask About the Products They Use

Professional hairdressers use professional-grade products – there's a significant difference between salon-quality colour and the kind you'd find on a supermarket shelf. Ask what brands they use for colouring, treatments and styling. Well-known professional brands include Wella, Alfaparf Milano, and L'Oréal Professionnel.

This matters particularly for colour services – professional colour gives more even, longer-lasting results and causes less damage to hair than box dyes.

Check Reviews and Testimonials

Word of mouth remains the gold standard for finding a trustworthy service provider. Ask friends, neighbours or family members in the Aylesbury area if they can recommend anyone. You can also check Google reviews, Facebook pages, or the hairdresser's website for testimonials from real clients.

Look for consistent positive feedback over time, particularly from clients with similar hair needs to yours. A hairdresser with many long-standing, repeat clients is a very good sign.

Discuss Flexibility and Availability

One of the key advantages of a mobile hairdresser is flexibility. A good mobile hairdresser should be able to accommodate your preferred appointment times, including evenings or weekends where possible. Discuss turnaround times for booking – during busy periods like wedding season or Christmas, availability can fill up quickly.

Have a Chat Before Booking

Before committing to an appointment, speak to the hairdresser directly. A phone call or message exchange is a great way to gauge how professional and personable they are, ask any questions about services and pricing, and establish whether you feel comfortable with them. A good mobile hairdresser will be happy to answer your questions and put you at ease before your first appointment.
