The Complete Guide to Keratin Treatments at Home

20 June 2026

If you struggle with frizzy, unmanageable hair, a keratin treatment could genuinely change your mornings. As one of the services I offer as a mobile hairdresser in Aylesbury, I carry out keratin treatments at clients' homes across Aylesbury – no need to visit a salon. Here's everything you need to know.

What Is a Keratin Treatment?

Keratin is a natural protein that makes up the structure of your hair. Over time – through heat styling, colouring, environmental exposure and general wear – this protein breaks down, leaving hair porous, frizzy and difficult to manage.

A professional keratin treatment infuses the hair with keratin protein, smoothing the cuticle and temporarily bonding the protein back into the hair structure. The result is smoother, shinier, more manageable hair that dries faster and requires less heat styling effort.

What Types of Keratin Treatment Are Available?

There are several different types of keratin smoothing treatments:

  • Brazilian Keratin Treatment (BKT): A highly effective smoothing treatment that can dramatically reduce frizz and add significant shine. Results typically last 3–5 months.
  • Express Keratin Treatment: A lighter version that takes less time and has a shorter result window (around 4–6 weeks). Good for first-timers or those with mild frizz.
  • Formaldehyde-free treatments: These use alternative smoothing agents and are suitable for clients who prefer to avoid formaldehyde-based formulas. Results may be slightly less dramatic but the treatment is gentler.

Who Is a Keratin Treatment Suited To?

Keratin treatments work best for:

  • Naturally curly or wavy hair that is prone to frizz
  • Colour-treated hair that has become porous and hard to manage
  • Anyone who spends a lot of time blow-drying or straightening and wants to reduce styling time
  • People with thick, coarse hair that needs taming

They are less suitable for very fine hair, as the treatment can sometimes weigh it down.

What Happens During the Treatment?

A keratin treatment appointment typically takes 2–3 hours, depending on hair length and thickness. Here's what to expect:

  1. Hair is washed with a clarifying shampoo to remove product build-up
  2. The keratin formula is applied to sections of damp hair
  3. Hair is blow-dried thoroughly to remove moisture
  4. A professional flat iron at high heat seals the treatment into the hair
  5. Some treatments require a final rinse and blow-dry to finish

Keratin Treatment Aftercare

The aftercare period is important for maximising results:

  • Avoid washing hair for 48–72 hours after the treatment
  • Use only sulphate-free shampoo and conditioner once washing resumes
  • Avoid tying hair up or using clips for the first 48 hours
  • Avoid swimming in chlorinated or salt water, which can reduce the treatment's longevity
  • The smoother and better-cared-for your hair, the longer the results will last
