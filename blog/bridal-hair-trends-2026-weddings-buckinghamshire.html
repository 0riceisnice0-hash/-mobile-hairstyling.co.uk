Bridal Hair Trends for 2026 Weddings in Aylesbury

Home›Blog›Bridal Hair Trends 2026

6 June 2026

2026 is shaping up to be a wonderful year for weddings, and bridal hair is no exception. Whether you're getting married in a grand country house, a cosy barn venue or a beautiful church, your hair should feel like you – elevated. As an experienced bridal hair stylist serving Aylesbury, here's what I'm seeing requested and loved this year.

Romantic Soft Waves

Loose, flowing waves remain perennially popular for brides and show no sign of fading in 2026. The appeal is obvious – they look effortlessly beautiful, suit a wide range of face shapes, and photograph stunningly in natural light. This year the trend leans towards slightly undone, natural-looking waves rather than overly polished curls, giving a romantic, relaxed feel that works beautifully for outdoor summer ceremonies.

The Modern Low Chignon

Elegant, timeless and incredibly flattering, the low chignon has experienced a real revival. The 2026 version is softer and less severe than its predecessors – think loose, textured twists with a few face-framing tendrils rather than a slick, tight bun. It's particularly beautiful when adorned with a delicate hairpiece or fresh flowers, which pairs wonderfully with beautiful garden venue settings.

Braided Details

Whether incorporated into an updo or worn as a standalone style, braids are having a significant moment in bridal hair. Micro braids woven into a half-up style, a simple fishtail braid for bohemian brides, or a braided crown for something more statement – there are countless ways to incorporate braiding into your wedding look in a way that feels modern and personal.

Half-Up, Half-Down Styles

The half-up, half-down style remains one of the most requested looks I see in 2026. It offers the best of both worlds – keeping hair away from the face while showing off length and texture. Variations include twisted half-ups, braided half-ups and voluminous half-ups with curled lengths below.

Accessories Are Bigger Than Ever

In 2026, bridal hair accessories have become a style statement in their own right. Pearl pins scattered through an updo, an oversized bow at the nape, a dramatic floral crown for outdoor venues, or a sleek embellished band – accessories are being used to personalise hairstyles and make them truly unique. See also: Wedding Hair Accessories: Choosing the Perfect Pieces.

Venue Considerations

The venue plays a surprisingly large role in hairstyle choice. A formal estate or country house tends to call for more polished, structured styles. A rustic barn or garden venue often suits more relaxed, romantic looks. Outdoor ceremonies need styles that can withstand weather – a well-secured updo is always more reliable than loose hair on a breezy summer day.

When you book a consultation with me, we'll talk through your venue and the overall feel of your day as part of finding the perfect style for you.
