Hairdressing at Home for Elderly & Less Mobile Clients

For many elderly people and those with limited mobility, a trip to the high street salon simply isn't practical. Whether it's difficulty getting around, health considerations, or simply the stress of navigating busy environments, the salon experience can feel out of reach. That's exactly where I come in.

I'm Harriet Bartley, a professional mobile hairdresser based in Aylesbury with over 21 years of experience. I visit clients in their own homes, care homes, residential homes and nursing homes throughout Aylesbury and the surrounding areas of Buckinghamshire. I bring everything needed for a full salon experience directly to you – all you need to do is sit back, relax and enjoy a lovely hair appointment in comfortable, familiar surroundings.

Over the years I've built a wonderful client base of elderly and less mobile clients who rely on me for regular, professional hair care. I understand the importance of taking time, being patient, and adapting my approach to each individual's needs and preferences.

Care Home Hairdresser – Aylesbury & Buckinghamshire

I regularly visit care homes, residential homes and nursing homes throughout the Aylesbury area to provide professional hairdressing for residents. I understand that hair care is not just about appearance – it plays a vital role in a person's self-esteem, dignity and overall wellbeing.

Many care home residents have their favourite hairstyle they've kept for decades. My goal is always to honour that, delivering the precise cut, set or colour they love, while making the appointment a positive, enjoyable part of their routine.

What Care Home Visits Include

  • Shampoo and condition (using a portable wash bowl where needed)
  • Cuts and trims – maintaining preferred styles with care and precision
  • Sets and blow-dries – classic styling that many elderly clients love
  • Perms – I offer traditional perming services which remain popular with older clients
  • Tints and grey coverage – maintaining colour confidently and safely
  • Grey blending – a modern alternative to full coverage colour
  • Scalp massage and conditioning treatments

I work with care home staff to schedule visits that fit around the home's routine and residents' appointments. Whether visiting one resident or a small group, I take the same professional care and thoroughness every time.

Home Hairdressing for Elderly Clients in Aylesbury

Visiting an elderly client at their own home is one of the most rewarding aspects of my work. Many of my long-standing clients have been with me for years – I know their hair, their preferences, and how they like to feel after their appointment. That continuity of care is something a high street salon simply cannot offer.

Why Home Visits Work So Well for Elderly Clients

There are many reasons why mobile hairdressing is the ideal solution for older clients:

  • No travel required – No need to arrange transport, navigate steps, or deal with the stress of going out. I come to your front door.
  • Familiar environment – Being in your own home means you're relaxed, comfortable and at ease throughout the appointment.
  • Unhurried service – I take as long as the appointment needs. There's no rushing to accommodate a busy salon schedule.
  • Full attention – You have my complete, undivided focus for the entire appointment. I get to know you and your hair properly over time.
  • Accessibility – Whether you use a wheelchair, walking frame, or find it difficult to sit in certain positions, I adapt my approach accordingly.
  • Post-surgery and health conditions – I'm experienced in working sensitively with clients recovering from illness, surgery or living with conditions affecting their comfort and posture.

Equipment and Products

I arrive fully equipped with everything required for the appointment – professional-grade shampoos, conditioners, colour products, cutting tools, dryers and styling equipment. I use quality professional products from brands including Wella, to ensure the best results while being gentle on hair and scalp. There's nothing for you to provide or prepare.

Services Available for Elderly Clients

I offer the full range of hair styling services to all of my elderly and less mobile clients, adapted as needed for comfort and practicality:

Cuts & Trims

Maintaining your preferred style with precision, whether that's a classic short set, a longer style or a smart trim. I listen carefully to exactly what you want.

Shampoo & Blow-dry

A relaxing shampoo, condition and professional blow-dry. I use a portable wash bowl to ensure comfortable shampooing, even for those with limited neck mobility.

Sets & Perms

Traditional roller sets and perms remain popular with many of my elderly clients. I offer these services with the same professional care at your home or care home as you would receive in a salon.

Colour & Grey Coverage

Tints, grey coverage and grey blending to maintain your colour confidently. I use professional-grade products for beautiful, long-lasting results.

Conditioning Treatments

Scalp massages and deep conditioning treatments to keep hair healthy, hydrated and looking its best between appointments.

Styling & Finishing

Whether it's a special occasion or simply wanting to feel well-groomed, I can create any style you have in mind – from simple and practical to elegant for a special event.
